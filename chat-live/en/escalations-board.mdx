The **Escalations Board** is the real-time operations hub for every conversation that has been escalated from the AI agent to a human specialist. It gives managers and team leads full visibility into open cases, assignment patterns, and specialist performance — all in one place.

## Accessing the Escalations Board

Navigate to **Contact Center > Live Chat > Escalations Board** in the left sidebar. You need the appropriate role permissions to view this page.

<Note>
If you don't have permission, the board displays a shield icon and a message indicating that access is restricted.
</Note>

---

## Page layout

At the top of the page you'll find:

- **Title and subtitle** describing the board's purpose.
- **View tabs** — switch between **Queue**, **Assignment**, and **Specialists** views directly from the header area.
- **Date controls** — a date range picker and quick-select pills (Today, 7D, 30D, 90D) that appear above the Queue and Assignment views.

---

## Queue view

The Queue is the default view and shows every escalated conversation in a sortable, searchable table.

### KPI cards

Four color-coded summary cards sit above the table:

| Card | Color | Description |
|---|---|---|
| **Open** | Bugambilia (pink) | Total open escalations |
| **Awaiting** | Yellow | Escalations with no human reply yet |
| **Overdue** | Orange | Escalations that have exceeded the response-time threshold |
| **Median 1st Response** | Blue | Median time to the first human reply |

<Tip>
Click the **Open**, **Awaiting**, or **Overdue** cards to instantly filter the table to that subset.
</Tip>

### Table columns

The table displays one row per escalated conversation:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | Contact name or external ID |
| **Channel** | The channel the conversation came from |
| **Status** | *Awaiting*, *Attended*, or *Resolved* |
| **Assigned to** | The specialist currently assigned |
| **Wait time** | How long the customer has been waiting |
| **Escalated at** | Timestamp of the escalation |

Use the **search bar** to find conversations by customer name or ID. Click any row to open the **conversation detail panel** on the right.

### Conversation detail panel

The slide-out panel shows:

- Customer name, status badge, and channel.
- Key timestamps — escalated at, first human reply, assigned to.
- Escalation reason (the AI agent's stated reason for handing off).
- **Reassign** button (managers only) to move the conversation to a different specialist.
- **Open Conversation** button to jump into the full live-chat view.

## Assignment view

The Assignment view visualizes how escalations are being distributed across the team over time.

### Assignment Timeline

A horizontal rail of cards representing past and upcoming assignments:

- **Past cards** (left of the NOW divider) — each card shows the specialist's avatar, name, the customer, assigned-by info, and when the assignment happened.
- **NOW divider** — a vertical pink rule with a pulsing dot marking the current moment.
- **Up Next cards** (right of the NOW divider) — show specialists in queue order. In **priority routing**, cards include the specialist's configured rank (e.g. *Rank #1*). The next specialist to receive an escalation is highlighted with a green border.

#### Specialist avatars

Each specialist is represented by a **notched-square avatar** (rounded with a squared top-right corner) showing their initials. A small status dot in the top-right corner indicates:

- 🟢 **Green** — online / available
- ⚫ **Grey** — offline / unavailable

Skipped or excluded specialists appear with dashed borders and muted styling.

#### Scrolling the timeline

Three controls sit in the top-right corner of the timeline card:

| Control | Action |
|---|---|
| **← (Back)** | Scroll left to see earlier assignments |
| **Now** | Re-center the rail on the current moment |
| **→ (Forward)** | Scroll right to see upcoming assignments |

The back and forward buttons disable automatically when you reach the beginning or end of the rail.

### Assignment Overview

Below the timeline, a unified **Assignment Overview** card presents two side-by-side columns with a dropdown to select the analysis window (**24 h**, **7 d**, or **30 d**):

| Column | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Distribution** (left) | Total assignments and a per-specialist bar chart showing how many escalations each specialist received. Each specialist has a consistent color across all surfaces. |
| **Response Times** (right) | Median first-response time (or average response time — toggle between them) and a per-specialist bar chart of response speed. |

<Tip>
Use the **First / Avg** toggle in the Response column to switch between median first-reply time and average response time per specialist.
</Tip>

## Specialists view

The Specialists view provides a per-member performance rollup over a selectable rolling window (**24 h**, **7 d**, or **30 d**).

For each specialist you can see:

- Number of escalations handled
- Median first-reply time
- Average handle time
- Current availability status

Managers can click a specialist row to view more detail or take actions such as reassignment.

---

## Specialist name display

Names are automatically normalized for readability:

- Names stored in ALL CAPS are converted to title case (e.g. *JUAN PÉREZ* → *Juan Pérez*).
- Extra whitespace is collapsed.
- In narrow columns, only the first name is shown.

---

## Color-coded specialists

Each specialist is assigned a consistent color from the platform's design palette. The same specialist will always appear in the same color across the timeline, distribution chart, and specialists tab — regardless of sort order.

---

## Key notes

- The Escalations Board requires the **escalation tool** to be enabled on your agent.
- The board refreshes data automatically; you can also change the date range or view to reload.
- Only users with the **manager** or equivalent role can reassign conversations.
- The response-time threshold (used to determine "overdue" status) is configured in your escalation tool settings.
