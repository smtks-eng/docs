The **Escalations Board** is the command center for managing conversations that have been escalated from your AI agent to human specialists. It provides real-time visibility into open escalations, assignment flow, and specialist workload—all in one place.

<Note>
  The Escalations Board requires the **Semantiks Contact Center** tool (`pass_control_semantiks_cc`) to be enabled on your agent. If it is not enabled, the board will display a prompt with a button to navigate to **Skills > Tools** where you can activate it.
</Note>

---

## Accessing the Escalations Board

Navigate to **Contact Center > Live Chat > Escalations Board** in the left sidebar. The board is gated by the `contact.escalations_board` permission on your organization—if you don't have access, you'll see a restricted-access message.

---

## Board Tabs

The Escalations Board is organized into three tabs at the top of the page:

| Tab | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Queue** | View and manage all escalated conversations, filter by status, search, and assign specialists |
| **Assignment** | See a timeline of recent assignments, routing logic, distribution stats, and response metrics |
| **Specialists** | Review per-specialist workload, performance, availability, and conversation history |

Switch between tabs using the tab bar at the top-right of the board.

---

## Date Range

All three tabs share a date range selector that controls the data window. You can:

- Pick a **preset** period: **24h**, **7d**, **30d**, or **90d**
- Select a **custom date range** using the date picker

The date range pills and custom picker appear above the content area in the Queue and Assignment tabs.

---

## Queue Tab

The Queue tab is the primary triage view. It displays all escalated conversations in a searchable, sortable table.

### KPI Cards

Four summary cards appear at the top:

| KPI | Description |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Total number of currently open escalated conversations |
| **Awaiting** | Conversations waiting for a specialist to respond |
| **Overdue** | Conversations that have exceeded the response threshold (highlighted in red when > 0) |
| **Median First Response** | Median time to first human reply across escalations |

### Filtering and Searching

Below the KPIs, the conversation table provides:

- **Search bar** — filter conversations by customer name, conversation ID, or other details
- **Status filter tabs** — quickly toggle between:
  - **Overdue** (default) — only conversations past the response threshold
  - **Awaiting** — conversations still waiting for a first reply
  - **All** — every open escalation regardless of status
- **Threshold selector** — set the overdue threshold in minutes (e.g., 15 min)

### Conversation Table

Each row shows:

| Column | Description |
|---|---|
| **Customer** | Name (or external ID) and conversation ID |
| **Channel** | The channel the conversation came from |
| **Status** | Badge showing Awaiting, Attended, or Resolved |
| **Assignee** | The specialist assigned (or "Unassigned") |
| **Waiting** | Time elapsed, shown with a color-coded progress bar — green if within threshold, red if overdue |

Click any row to open the **conversation detail panel** on the right side.

### Conversation Detail Panel

When you click a conversation row, a side panel slides open with:

- **Customer info** — name, external ID, channel
- **Conversation ID** — with a copy button
- **Status badge** and escalation timestamp
- **Timeline** — escalated at, first human message (if any), and waiting time
- **Actions**:
  - **Open conversation** — navigate to the full conversation view
  - **Unassign** — remove the current specialist assignment
  - **Assign / Reassign** — open the reassignment dialog to pick a specialist

<Tip>
  Managers see a highlighted "Reassign" button when a conversation already has an assignee, making it easy to redistribute workload.
</Tip>

### Reassigning a Conversation

The reassignment dialog shows all organization members. You can:

- Search by name
- See who is currently assigned (highlighted)
- Select a new specialist and confirm

## Assignment Tab

The Assignment tab gives you visibility into **how** escalations are being routed and **who** is receiving them.

### Assignment Timeline

A horizontally scrollable timeline shows recent escalation assignments in chronological order (oldest on the left, newest on the right). Each card displays:

- **When** the escalation happened
- **Specialist** assigned (with avatar)
- **Conversation ID** (with copy button)
- **Assigned by** — whether the AI agent or a human manager made the assignment
- **First reply** — timestamp of the specialist's first response, or a "No reply yet" indicator

A pulsing **NOW** marker separates past assignments from upcoming ones.

#### Up-Next Cards

To the right of the NOW marker, the timeline shows **who will receive the next escalations** based on the current routing configuration:

- If **priority routing** is enabled, cards appear in priority order with rank labels ("Next up", "+2", "+3", etc.). Unavailable or excluded specialists appear grayed out with a reason.
- If priority routing is not enabled, available specialists are shown without rank labels.

### Distribution & Response Metrics

Below the timeline, two cards show aggregated stats with their own time window selector (**24h**, **7d**, **30d**):

| Card | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Assignment Distribution** | How escalations are distributed across specialists — bar chart showing each specialist's share |
| **Response Metrics** | Aggregated response time statistics — median first response, average response time, and SLA compliance rate |

---

## Specialists Tab

The Specialists tab provides a per-specialist breakdown of workload and performance.

### Specialist List

A scrollable list on the left shows all specialists with:

- **Name** and avatar
- **Availability status** (online/offline)
- **Conversation count** in the selected period
- **Performance summary** — response times and resolution rate

Click any specialist to see their detailed view on the right.

### Specialist Detail View

When a specialist is selected, the right panel shows:

- **Conversation history** — recent escalated conversations handled by this specialist
- **Performance metrics** — first response time, resolution time, and customer satisfaction
- **Availability** — current online/offline status

The Specialists tab has its own time window selector (**24h**, **7d**, **30d**) and respects the overdue threshold configured in the Queue tab.

---

## Loading States

The Escalations Board uses skeleton loading states throughout — KPI cards, table rows, timeline cards, and specialist lists all display animated placeholders while data is being fetched, giving you immediate visual feedback.

---

## Key Notes

- The board requires the **Semantiks Contact Center** tool to be enabled. If it's not active, you'll see a prompt to enable it from **Skills > Tools**.
- Access is controlled by the `contact.escalations_board` organization permission.
- The overdue threshold (default: 15 minutes) applies across all tabs.
- Data refreshes automatically when you change date ranges, filters, or tab views.
