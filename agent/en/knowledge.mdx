## Adding Knowledge

If you are here you already **finished the onboarding**  and have your console ready to set-up. The first step would be adding some knowledge to the agent

**Knowledge is a key component of your agent.**\
The agent will only respond based on the information available in your knowledge base. This means that adding irrelevant data or lacking essential information can lead to a poor user experience. That's why setting up your knowledge base correctly is one of the most critical steps when configuring your agent.

The Knowledge page is organized into tabs:

- **FAQs** — manage your frequently asked questions.
- **Files** — upload documents for the agent to learn from.
- **Links** — extract knowledge from web pages.
- **Glossaries** — define terms and definitions so your agent uses consistent, accurate vocabulary. See the dedicated [Glossaries](/agent/en/glossaries) page for full details.
- **Vector Vault** — manage your vector storage.

## FAQs

The FAQs tab lets you create, organize, and manage the frequently asked questions your agent uses to respond to users. FAQs are grouped by **topics**, and each FAQ consists of a question and an answer.

### Filtering FAQs by escalation

If your FAQs include entries that are configured to **escalate to a human agent**, you can quickly isolate them using the **Escalations** filter button in the toolbar.

- Click the **Escalations** button (identified by a person icon) to show **only** the FAQs that have the escalation flag enabled.
- The button displays a count in parentheses showing how many FAQs in the current topic (or across all topics) are set to escalate.
- Click the button again to remove the filter and see all FAQs.

<Tip>
  Use the Escalations filter together with the search bar and topic selector to quickly audit which questions hand off to a live agent.
</Tip>

## Files — Generating FAQs from documents

The **Files** tab lets you upload documents (PDF, DOCX, TXT, etc.) and automatically generate FAQs from their content. Once generated, you can review, edit, select, and save the FAQs into your knowledge base.

### Similarity detection

When FAQs are generated from a file, the platform automatically checks them against your **existing knowledge base** — including FAQs, glossary entries, and documents — and surfaces any similar content it finds. This helps you avoid duplicates and keep your knowledge base clean.

#### How it works

Each generated FAQ is compared to existing entries using semantic similarity. If a match is found above the relevance threshold, a **colored badge** appears next to the generated FAQ's question:

| Badge color | Similarity | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Green** | 70 % or higher | Very similar — likely a duplicate or near-duplicate |
| **Amber** | 45 % – 69 % | Moderately similar — worth reviewing |

Matches below 45 % are not surfaced.

The badge shows how many similar existing entries were found (e.g., **"2 similar"**). Click the badge to open a **popover panel** with full details about each match, including:

- The **type** of existing content (FAQ, Glossary, or Document)
- The **similarity score** as a percentage
- The **title** and **content preview** of the existing entry

#### Actions on similar matches

From the similarity popover you can take action on each match:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="Replace">
    Click **Replace** on an existing FAQ match to mark the generated FAQ as a replacement. When you save, the generated FAQ will **overwrite** the existing one instead of creating a new entry.

    - Only one generated FAQ can replace a given existing FAQ at a time.
    - When a replace is active, the row is highlighted in blue and the badge shows a **replace** indicator.
    - Click **Will replace** again to undo.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Delete old">
    Click **Delete old** on an existing FAQ match to mark it for removal. When you save, that existing FAQ will be **deleted** from your knowledge base.

    - You can mark multiple existing entries for deletion.
    - When marked, a red **delete** indicator appears on the badge.
    - Click **Will delete** again to undo.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Discard">
    Click **Discard this generated FAQ** at the bottom of the popover to remove the generated FAQ from the review table entirely — useful when the existing entry is already good enough.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

<Note>
  **Replace** and **Delete old** are mutually exclusive for the same existing entry. Selecting one automatically deselects the other.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Use similarity detection to keep your knowledge base lean: replace outdated answers with freshly generated ones, delete entries that are no longer relevant, or discard generated FAQs that duplicate content you already have.
</Tip>

## Knowledge Review

The **Knowledge Review** is an AI-powered audit tool that scans your agent's entire knowledge base—FAQs, glossary entries, communication instructions, and special instructions—and returns actionable suggestions to improve quality and consistency.

### Opening the Knowledge Review

Click the **✨ (sparkle) icon** in the top-right area of the header bar. The icon appears whenever you have an agent selected. A sidebar panel slides open on the right side of the screen.

<Tip>
  The review button is available from **any page** inside the agent section—not just the Knowledge page—so you can trigger an audit at any time.
</Tip>

### How it works

1. **Run the review** — When you open the sidebar for the first time (or click **Run review again**), the platform sends your agent's knowledge to an AI model that analyzes it for issues.
2. **Review suggestions** — The AI returns a list of suggestions. Each suggestion includes:
   - A **title** summarizing the issue
   - A **description** explaining what's wrong and why it matters
   - An **action link** that takes you to the exact place in the console where you can fix it
3. **Apply fixes** — Click a suggestion's action link to navigate directly to the relevant page and field. Some links deep-link into specific sections (e.g., a particular communication instruction).

### Suggestion categories

Suggestions are organized by the area of the knowledge base they affect:

| Category | What it covers |
|---|---|
| **FAQs** | Duplicate questions, vague answers, missing topics, inconsistencies between Q&A pairs |
| **Glossary** | Undefined terms used in FAQs, conflicting definitions, terms that should be added |
| **Communication Instructions** | Contradictory instructions, instructions that are too vague, or missing guidelines |
| **Special Instructions** | Issues with the free-text special instructions field on the Identity page |

### Tips for getting the most out of Knowledge Review

<Tip>
  Run a review **after bulk-importing FAQs** or making significant changes to your glossary. The AI is especially good at catching inconsistencies introduced by large edits.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  You don't need to fix every suggestion at once. Use the review as a checklist and work through items over time.
</Tip>

<Note>
  The Knowledge Review analyzes a snapshot of your knowledge at the time you run it. If you make changes after running a review, click **Run review again** to get updated suggestions.
</Note>
